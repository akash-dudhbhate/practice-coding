# Study Schedule — 12-Week Mastery Plan

> **Goal:** Complete all 100 lessons + 34 projects in 12 weeks (84 days).
> **Daily commitment:** 3–4 hours/day (can split into 2 sessions).
> **Pace:** ~1 lesson/day + problems, with projects on weekends.
> **Adapt:** If you have more time, do 2 lessons/day. If less, extend to 16–20 weeks.

---

## How to Use This Schedule

1. **Each day** = 1 lesson (read concepts → solve 9 problems → check with coding-check.md)
2. **Weekends** = project builds (apply the week's lessons to a sellable project)
3. **Milestones** = checkpoints to assess readiness before moving on
4. **Buffer days** = catch up, review, or get ahead
5. **Rule:** Don't skip ahead. Each lesson builds on the previous one.

## Tips for Success

1. **Don't skip the concepts.md** — it explains WHY, not just WHAT. Understanding why prevents memorization.
2. **Solve all 9 problems** — easy reinforces, medium builds logic, hard combines concepts.
3. **Check with coding-check.md** — verify your solution meets all requirements before moving on.
4. **Build the projects** — projects are where theory becomes skill. They're also sellable.
5. **If stuck:** re-read concepts.md, review previous lessons, search the web, then ask.
6. **If ahead:** start the next lesson or build an extra project.
7. **If behind:** don't rush. Better to understand 1 lesson fully than skim 3.
8. **Track progress:** run `python progress.py` to see your completion stats.
